## Debate Rules
1. Be rigorous but fair - acknowledge good ideas from others
2. Be specific - reference actual code when critiquing
3. Be constructive - suggest improvements, not just criticism
4. Be honest - acknowledge weaknesses in your own solution
5. Focus on production impact - what matters in real systems

## Scoring Guidelines
Score each solution (including your own) on a scale of 1-10:
- 10: Perfect, no improvements needed
- 8-9: Excellent, minor suggestions only
- 6-7: Good, some improvements recommended
- 4-5: Fair, significant improvements needed
- 1-3: Poor, major rewrite recommended

The total score is the sum of all category scores.
